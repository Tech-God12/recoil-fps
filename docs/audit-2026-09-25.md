## 1. Gunfeel: recoil, ADS, sway/bob, muzzle, hit feedback, reload, sound, falloff, TTK

- **G1 (S2). ADS still snaps with no settle overshoot.** `engine.ts` ADS lerp: `ads += (target-ads)*(1-exp(-dt*rate))` (rate 14.5 hip, 18 ADS). Critically damped, zero overshoot by design. Real optics have a tiny inertia overshoot + settle that tells the brain “glass seated”. *Why:* scopes feel like a UI tween, not a physical mass. Evidence: `engine.ts:3849` ADS block. Fix (S): add 6–8% under-damped spring (second-order, `vel += (target-pos)*k - vel*damping`) for the first 120 ms, tested headless for no centre drift.
- **G2 (S2). Viewmodel weapon kick is halved in ADS but camera kick is not.** `vmKick*0.45` in viewmodel, but `recoilP/Y` → `camera.rotation` is undamped by ADS except via `RECOIL_FLOORS`. So firing ADS jolts the world more than the gun, breaking the “sight stays on target” read. *Why:* ADS should feel *more* stable, not less. Evidence: `animateViewmodel: kickM=1-a*0.55` vs `recoilImpulse: aiming?0.92:1`. Fix (S): scale camera `recoilP/Y` by `1-a*0.35` as well, or expose `adsRecoilMul` per weapon family.
- **G3 (S3). Sprint lowers the gun but sprint-to-fire delay is invisible.** `sprintPose` dips `+0.08x -0.13y` over 150 ms; `sprintToFireDelay=0.2 s` blocks shots, but HUD gives no “weapon not ready” cue. Player mashes fire and feels unresponsive. *Why:* CoD shows a tiny centre-line block or sound. Evidence: `engine.ts:3883`, `animateViewmodel: sprintPose`. Fix (S): grey-out crosshair / show 200 ms “SPRINT” tick in HUD, or simply allow the first shot to cancel sprint faster (0.2→0.12 s).
- **G4 (S3). Bolt-action forces unscoped after every shot (AWM).** `tryFire` sets `rmb=false` for bolt guns. Authentic, but with 1.25 s cycle + re-ADS the felt TTK is ~2 s and the player never learns the rifle’s actual recoil. Evidence: `engine.ts` bolt handling. Fix (S): keep-ADS option (fire keeps `rmb` if the player is still holding it; release still unscopes). Needs player test, but headless can pin timing.
- **G5 (S2). No bullet crack / near-miss snap.** Enemy misses are silent except the distant muzzle report; supersonic crack when a round passes within ~4 m of the ear is 50% of “being shot at” feel and teaches direction. *Why:* players die to invisible fire with no directional cue. Evidence: `audio.ts` has `burstDirect` but no `crack` path; `ai.ts` fire has no near-miss hook. Fix (M): on enemy miss, if ray passes within 4 m of `eyePos`, play a panned crack (`pan = sign(cross)`).
- **G6 (S2). Single shared hitmarker lacks head vs body read.** `Hud.tsx` draws one `hm` / `hm-kill` shape; headshots already `dink`, but the visual is identical. Competitors use a distinct headshot confirm (red diamond/double-tick). *Why:* the shooter can’t learn “I’m only body-shotting” without staring at the feed. Evidence: `Hud.tsx:269`. Fix (S): head hitmark = rotated square + louder `dink` + 5% larger spread pulse.
- **G7 (S3). Reload choreography per family is tasty, but timings are not surfaced.** `reloadDur` varies, but HUD only shows a bar; the *feel* of AK rock-and-lock vs AR chest-height is not taught. Evidence: `animateViewmodel` 6 branches. Fix (S): per-family audio stagger already exists; add a 60 ms magazine-seat *thunk* synced to `rock` phase (already has `rock` var) so the ear confirms the hand.

## 2. Weapon models: silhouette, proportions, materials, details, sights, attachment fit

- **W1 (S2). Viewmodel is still ~47k tris / 30 draws (M4 probe §6) — a third of a frame.** `GunBuilder` cylinders use 18–24 segments even on hidden faces; each gun merges per-material but still carries 5–7 drawables (darkSteel/wood/rubber/steel…). *Why:* a first-person gun should be 10–15k tris; this one costs more than some interiors and re-renders every frame at full rate. Evidence: `weapons/geometry.ts` `cyl` seg 20+, `weapons/rifles.ts` per-gun counts. Fix (M): decimate pass on cylinders (hidden ends 6–8 seg), merge static parts per material where finish allows, pin per-gun tri budgets headless (M249 44 draws → ≤28).
- **W2 (S2). Hands are procedural boxes, not gloves.** `attachArms` builds RoundedBox gloves + 8-sided sleeves; from the screenshots the “fingers” read as a mitten. The right hand is static even during reload slaps. *Why:* the single biggest “prototype” tell in every screenshot after the viewmodel scale fix. Evidence: `weapons/core.ts:129-190` `gloveBox` + `limb`. Fix (M): add knuckle creases, thumb separation, and a reload-specific right-hand micro-pose (thumb releases mag catch for AK). Needs a browser, but geometry budget can be pinned headless.
- **W3 (S2). Material response is uniform.** All steels share `roughness 0.62–0.78, metalness 0.62–0.95`; polymer is `0.78/0.10`; wood is `0.58`. In screenshots `kasbah-combat.png` the M4 is a black silhouette because the single warm key + no fill makes metal read as matte plastic. *Why:* metal vs polymer vs walnut must feel different or the armory looks cheap. Evidence: `weapons/core.ts WM.*`, `engine.ts vmScene` lights (hemi + warm sun + cool fill, but fill is 0.45 intensity). Fix (S): raise tungsten/steel micro-scratch via `finish()` normal variance, lower polymer metalness to 0.04, add a camera-side cool rim to `vmScene` (already 0.45, bump to 0.65 for 20 frames after fire for readability).
- **W4 (S2). Sights alignment is trusted, not tested.** `sightY` per gun 0.051–0.097 plus `optic.userData.sightYOffset`; ADS math is `-(sightY+offset)*S`, but no headless assertion proves the bore line meets screen centre per weapon/optic/mag combo. *Why:* a single mis-seated reticle breaks trust in all optics. Evidence: `weapons/core.ts sightY`, `engine.ts animateViewmodel` `adsX/Y`. Fix (S): headless ADS-alignment test per catalog gun + worst-case fitted optic, assert bore ray within 0.6° of camera forward.
- **W5 (S3). Rail accessories float or clip.** Compact rail lasers seat against a `rail` slot at `[-.030, .004, -.320]` but the laser mesh is a 4-unit box; screenshots show it hovering 2 mm above the handguard on M4 vs flush on AK. Evidence: `weapons/rifles.ts rail(...)` + `attachments.ts` `laser*`. Fix (S): per-weapon rail `y` micro-offset table + snap laser housing to the picatinny lug, not the bore line; pin fit IDs in `weapon-assembly.test.js`.
- **W6 (S3). Ejection port / selector / charging handle are inconsistent across families.** AK selector is a flat profile; AR selector is a tiny cylinder; AWM bolt is a 6-mm stub. Real guns have distinct signatures (AK’s long sweep, AR’s 90° lever, AWM’s oversized knob). Evidence: `weapons/rifles.ts` `selector lever`, `compact.ts`. Fix (M): silhouette checklist per gun vs reference photos, one gun at a time.

## 3. Enemy AI: readability, cover, flanking, reaction, grenades, dumb moments, death

- **A1 (S1). Full-squad instant knowledge still.** `Squad.alertAll` + `shareIntel` give every patrol-state bot your exact `lastKnown` the frame one bot sees you (reaction 0.5 s Normal, 0.28 Hard). Combined with ~18 squad DPS, being spotted in the open = death in ~2 s with no counterplay except breaking LOS instantly. *Why:* feels like wallhacks, not tactics. Evidence: `ai.ts:354,527,535`, `tdm.ts:993`. Fix (M): intel delay (radio 1.0–2.5 s by difficulty), imperfect shared position (`lastKnown + errorRadius 3–7 m`), pin with a 100-round sim test that “you are not shot from behind cover in <1.5 s”.
- **A2 (S2). Intent is unreadable.** No stance change, hand raise, or HUD compass ping distinguishes PATROL/FLANK/generic ENGAGE; grenades arrive with only a radio line, no throw-anim / arm raise. *Why:* deaths feel random; good AI telegraphs. Evidence: `ai.ts doFlank/doAdvance` vs `Hud.tsx` compass (no flank pin). Fix (M): flankers raise a hand / HUD “FLANK” ping; grenade thrower must peek/stand for 0.35 s + play pin-pull spatially at their feet before the arc.
- **A3 (S2). Grenades can come through cover with no tell and be silent past 12 m.** `tryGrenade` targets `lastKnown + jitter` with no peek check; HUD grenade arrow only shows <12 m. *Why:* “exploded with no warning” deaths are the #1 rage quit. Evidence: `ai.ts:686 tryGrenade`, `engine.ts grenadeAngle` (<12 m). Fix (S): force thrower to have LOS to the cook point or a nav-visible arc; always show grenade chevron on compass when a frag is in-air within 30 m.
- **A4 (S2). Flank/advance goals are not nav-clamped at creation.** `flankTarget = pp + perp*16 + away*4` is raw geometry; `goTo` recovers via `nearestFree`, but the first 1.5 s path may be a U-turn around a wall corner, visible to the player. Evidence: `ai.ts:659 doFlank`. Fix (S): run `nearestFree` on the chosen `flankTarget` at creation; assert no 120°+ initial correction in a sim.
- **A5 (S2). Soldiers can stare through walls.** `checkLOS` tests `lastKnown` without a wall probe for the *body* position check; they strafe into a wall while still “having LOS” because the ray is eye-to-eye. Evidence: `ai.ts:309 checkLOS` (fwd vector + raycast but no body-width test). Fix (S): thicken LOS by testing two offset eyes (±0.25 m lateral) and require one clear.
- **A6 (S2). Corpses never ragdoll; deaths are 1.1–1.3 s canned falls (already improved with reactions.ts, but still procedural, no physics).** With the new 5-variant system (crumple/back/forward/spin/kneel/blast) it no longer reads “plank”, but every headshot crumples identically and no limb obeys gravity after settle. *Why:* repetition kills the reward after ~20 kills. Evidence: `reactions.ts VARIANTS`. Fix (M): add 2–3 timing/angle jitters per variant + a post-settle gravity nudge so limbs don’t freeze mid-air; true ragdoll needs a physics engine (out of scope, no deps).
- **A7 (S3). No visible “I’m aiming at you” tell for designated marksmen / AWM bots.** Players can’t learn to break LOS before the 0.28 s Hard reaction. Fix (S): laser / glint only while ADS + within 7° of player eye, fades beyond 40 m. Not shipping this session (scope creep).

Landmarks exist in data but are never labelled in-world or on the compass.** `world.landmarks` (water tower, souk, keep…) and `SIROCCO_OPEN` names feed nothing — no 3D markers, no compass strip labels. *Why:* callouts (“souk courtyard”) mean nothing until memorised; new players are lost. ## 5. Graphics: lighting, shadows, tone mapping, fog, bloom, grading, post, LOD, pacing

- **R1 (S1). Even after the fixes, the worst-case GPU spike is still “every 10th frame re-renders the full shadow map”.** `renderer.shadowMap.autoUpdate=false`, `needsUpdate=true` on frame 1, 2, and every 10th frame + on-demand. Median tris 105k → max 231k is 2.2× (Sandblast). Six hitches per second is what “laggy” feels like even at 60 fps average. *Why:* micro-stutter > average FPS for perceived smoothness. Evidence: `engine.ts:4438`. Fix (M): keep the 10-frame cadence but also skip the shadow pass if no actor moved >0.8 m since last shadow frame (dirty-flag).
- **R2 (S2). No LOD/culling beyond frustum for persistent meshes.** 260 pooled decal meshes + 60 blood meshes are always in the graph (`visible=false` still traversed); soldiers are full-detail at 200 m even though they’re 12 pixels tall. *Why:* graph traversal + draw-call setup on every frame; low-end cliff later. Evidence: `effects.ts holes/bloods`, `models.ts buildSoldier` (6 meshes per soldier, no distance LOD except rifle swap). Fix (S): `InstancedMesh` for decals (2 draws total) + distance LOD for soldiers (14 m rifle swap already ships; extend to body `layers` or impostor past 60 m).
- **R3 (S2). Post chain still compiles and sizes bloom even when `postFxOn=false`.** `UnrealBloomPass` is constructed, `composer.setSize` runs each resize, and `vignettePass.enabled=false` still costs a pass slot — but the direct-render path already skips passes, so this is construction cost + one extra size sync per resize. Minor, but the 2026-09-24-b fix (“render straight to canvas”) left the growth in place. Evidence: `engine.ts:832 bloom`, `1503 setSize`, `board`. Fix (S): lazy-construct `bloom` only when first enabled; or at least don’t `composer.addPass(bloom)` when off.
- **R4 (S2). Shadow texels are still ~10 cm on 1024 (50 m box, 1024).** Follow-cam is 1.6× sharper than the old 80 m box, but soft, crawling blobs remain under 1024. The user’s “laggy” includes “soft shadows swim”. Evidence: `shadow-fit.ts SHADOW_SPAN=50`, `snapToShadowTexels`. Fix (S): expose a “high” 2048 toggle in Settings performance section with a one-line cost note; already exists as `shadowQuality medium/high` but guidance is weak.
- **R5 (S3). Terrain receives shadows but is now receive-only.** Good (saves 36k shadow tris). Keep.
- **R6 (S3). Tone mapping ACES + 1.04 contrast was removed with the CSS vignette move.** The contrast nudge disappeared when post is off; exposure is now 1.05 with no subtle crush. Evidence: `engine.ts:750-751`. Fix (S): bake a 1.02 exposure lift when vignette>0, or accept — not shipping (needs eyes).
- **R7 (S3). Render resolution scaler is invisible when it moves.** FPS chip now shows `· 85%` when adaptive steps down, but nothing explains *why* it stepped or how to raise it. Evidence: `Hud.tsx fps-chip`. Fix (S): tooltip on hover “adaptive resolution — raise resolution scale or lower shadows to hold 100%”.

## 6. HUD/UI: hierarchy, contrast, density, dead pixels, timing, tokens, missing modern FPS elements

- **U1 (S1). HUD polls engine state and re-renders the whole React tree at 20 Hz.** `App.tsx:237` polls `hud()` every 50 ms and reconciles the full HUD (compass 73 nodes + feed + radar + ammo). Engine `hud()` is cheap (0.01–0.05 ms), but React reconciliation of the full tree at 20 Hz is a few ms of main-thread time competing with `rAF`. *Why:* jank on mid laptops even when JS FPS looks fine. Evidence: `App.tsx useEffect hud poll`, probe `hudMs 0.03`. Fix (M): split fast (crosshair/compass 30 Hz) vs slow (scoreboard/feed 4 Hz) channels, or memoize static sub-trees.
- **U2 (S2). No killcam / death recap beyond damage arcs.** Death shows arcs only; no “killed by X with Y from Zm” recap, no replay camera. *Why:* every modern FPS gives a 3 s learn moment; without it, deaths feel arbitrary. Evidence: `engine.ts damagePlayer*`, `Hud.tsx dmgArcs`. Fix (L): 4-s death cam (killer’s eye + weapon tag + distance) — out of scope this session, but the hook (`lastDamageFrom`) already exists.
- **U3 (S3). No hit-location feedback on the shooter side beyond the head “dink”.** Body/limb hits share one tick; the new death variants partly cover this visually but the shooter’s *instant* feedback is still mono. Evidence: `effects.ts impact` one colour, `audio.ts headshot dink` only. Fix (S): limb thud variant + slightly larger head hitmark (covered in G6).
- **U4 (S3). Compass pings stack without a limit read.** `pings.length>14 shift`, `age 3.2 s`; at 14 the strip is a solid bar. Evidence: `engine.ts pings`. Fix (S): cluster pings within 12° into one brighter ping.
- **U5 (S3). Settings has presets but no per-knob cost callout.** Presets + one-line “biggest levers” note fix guidance; individual sliders still don’t show cost. Evidence: `Settings.tsx` Performance section. Fix (S): tiny “cost: high/low” tag under resolution/shadows/bloom rows.
- **U6 (S2). Ammo-type is never shown.** HUD shows `mag/magSize` + weapon name, but not calibre/shotgun-vs-rifle visual distinction; players can’t learn pellet vs bullet identity without firing. Evidence: `Hud.tsx` ammo block. Fix (S): show `CAL 7.62 / PELLET x8` under the mag, driven by `weaponById`.

## 7. Audio: mix, positional, footsteps, layers, music/stingers

- **D1 (S2). No body-fall sound beyond a 0.4 s thud; no weapon clatter variance.** Corpses now `bodyFallSpatial` + `weaponClatterSpatial` through HRTF, but heavy vs light is binary and the mic distance rolloff is the same for both. *Why:* a 15 m fall should sound different from a 2 m crumple. Evidence: `reactions.ts` `onBodyFall` `heavy` boolean, `engine.ts deathAudio`. Fix (S): heavy = blast/crumple velocity >0.3, light otherwise, plus a 120 ms delay for the rifle tumble to sell air time.
- **D2 (S2). Enemy footsteps are not positional (only the player’s).** `audio.footstep` exists for the player; enemies are silent movers outside the 14 m gunshot notify radius. *Why:* flanking is silent, which is “wallhack” in reverse (you can’t hear the flank). Evidence: `ai.ts` has no enemy footstep hook. Fix (M): drive `audio.footstep` from `Enemy.footPhase` when within 18 m, with AI anim hook, throttled 0.45 s.
- **D3 (S2). Missing layers: ricochets, shell casings double-play, cloth, breathing.** Player footstep traps exist; no casing tink on reload/fire (only the InstancedMesh visual); no ricochet whine on metal impacts; no cloth/breathing under sprint. *Why:* each is a small realism sale; casing tick is the cheapest “I am shooting a machine” cue. Evidence: `effects.ts ejectBrass` has no audio companion except a faint `tink` timer in `audio.ts` that is rarely triggered. Fix (S): ring a soft `tink` 80 ms after each `ejectBrass` (already has `lastEjectT` but not wired).
- **D4 (S3). Voice = OS text-to-speech.** Robot announcer with per-machine voices; enemy barks are TTS too. Why: inconsistent quality, can feel placeholder; but zero-asset and it *works*. Keep; note as asset-ceiling, not a bug. Evidence: `voice.ts` `speechSynthesis`. Not shipping — needs recorded assets.
- **D5 (S3). `spatialVoices` expiry.** `expires` timestamps exist; `updateListener` prunes, but a 24-voice cap + `size>=24` hard-delete can evict a live one-shot mid-tail. Evidence: `audio.ts:93`. Fix (S): cap at 28, or LRU by `expires`.

## 8. Game feel & juice: shake, hitstop, camera kick, slow-mo, kill confirm, streaks, victory

- **J1 (S2). Kills land with hitstop + crosshair pulse + directional flinch (session A/B), but screen shake is per-frame RNG.** `composeCamera` uses raw `Math.random()` offsets — jittery rather than punchy, and unseeded (untestable). *Why:* shake should have trauma decay (smooth noise with a curve), not white noise. Evidence: `engine.ts:4154 shake`. Fix (S): trauma-based shake (`shake *= exp(-dt*…)` + perlin, or at least `Math.sin(time*…)*shake`).
- **J2 (S2). Victory/defeat sequences are thin.** Win = voice line + 0.6 s delay + debrief screen; no slow-mo final kill, no music sting, no MVP framing beyond a star in the Tab board. *Why:* the emotional payoff of 10 minutes is a spreadsheet. Evidence: `engine.ts pendingResult`, `Screens.tsx ResultsScreen`. Fix (M): final-kill slow-mo (0.3× for 1.2 s) + sting + graded stamp animation (stamp exists — animate it). Out of scope for the lag-focused session.
- **J3 (S3). Screen shake on explosions ignores occlusion.** `shake = min(1.4, 14/dist)` even if 3 walls block the blast; a mortar behind the kasbah keep shakes you the same as one at your feet. Evidence: `engine.ts:2590,2787,3237`. Fix (S): scale shake by 0.4 if `freeLength` < blast distance (wall blocks air).
- **J4 (S3). Streak callouts can collide with mission banners (`fx.banner && !fx.missionBanner` hides streaks during objectives — the nuke banner can be swallowed).** Evidence: `Hud.tsx streak`. Fix (S): queue banners, never drop. Not shipping (needs banner-queue design).

## 11. Performance & robustness: FPS on mid hardware, GC, leaks, context loss, resize, tab-away

- **F1 (S1). The “laggy” report — root causes R1, R2, M2, W1, plus two unshipped perf leaks below.** Headline this session. See producer cut.
- **F2 (S2). `Effects` decals are 260 scene-graph nodes, always traversed.** `new THREE.Mesh(CircleGeometry)` ×200 holes + ×60 bloods, each toggled `visible`; `visible=false` still costs graph traversal + matrix update. - **F3 (S2). HUD React tree reconciles at 20 Hz with 73 compass nodes + full tree.** Engine `hud()` is cheap (0.01–0.05 ms per `perf-probe`), but React reconciliation of the full HUD at 20 Hz on a mid laptop is a few ms competing with `rAF`. Evidence: `App.tsx` hud poll. Fix (M): split fast/slow HUD channels (see U1) + memoize static sub-trees (`MissionObjective`, compass ticks).
- **F4 (S2). `hud()` allocates per poll (`pings.map`, `enemiesMap.map/filter`, roster spreads).** 20 allocations ×20 Hz = 400 objects/s. Minor individually, but a GC tick every few seconds is exactly the micro-stutter the player feels as “lag”. Evidence: `engine.ts:4624 hud()` spreads. Fix (S): reuse buffers or accept in this session; measure first. **→ part of headline.**
- **F5 (S3). Viewmodel scene compiles 5 full guns at boot.** `vmScene.add(m4,ak,m1911,awm,mp7)` + `compileAsync` for each; `buildWorld` 450 ms + PMREM + compile is staged but still blocks the first paint on low-end. - **F7 (S3). Resize / blur-pause / context-loss are handled but untested under the new adaptive scaler.** Blur pauses the sim; adaptive resumes on focus. Needs a real browser resize test. Keep.

## Session plan (producer cut)

The player asked for **two big things**, plus “make it not laggy”. They explicitly forbid kits. Two headlines, not one, plus a polish batch whose collective “it feels finished” is the real product.

### Headline A — Frame Budget II: finish the lag (S1: R1/R2, F1/F2/F3, U1, C1)  *(the “optimize” promise)*

The 2026-09-24 frame-budget pass cut shader lights, post, shadows and bot rifles — median draw calls 203→165, tris 143k→105k, but the “laggy” report persists because the biggest traversals were not touched. This headline finishes it:

1. **Instanced decals (F2):** 200 bullet holes + 60 bloods go from 260 individual `Mesh`es (260 graph nodes, 260 matrix updates, 260 draw setups) to **2 `InstancedMesh`es** (hole + blood). One draw each, zero cost when hidden, `instanceMatrix.needsUpdate` only on the touched index. Also caps `bloodDecalMat` overdraw — today every blood is an additive 10-segment `CircleGeometry` even when hidden.
2. **HUD channel split (U1/F3/F4):** `App.tsx` polls `engine.hud()` at 20 Hz and re-renders the full React tree (compass 73 nodes, radar, feed, ammo). Split into **fast (crosshair/compass/reticle 30 Hz)** vs **slow (feed/scoreboard/mission banner 4 Hz)**; memoize `MissionObjective` + compass tick sub-trees; make `hud()` allocation-free on the fast path (reuse a scratch `HudState` and only `map` on the slow tick).
3. **Shadow dirty-flag (R1):** keep the 10-frame cadence, but **skip the shadow re-render if no actor moved >0.8 m and the player moved <0.5 m** since the last shadow frame. Six hitches/s become one hitch/s when holding an angle — exactly when the player is trying to aim.
4. **GC pass (F4/C1):** `hud()` stops allocating `pings.map`/`enemiesMap.map` every 50 ms; `checkLOS`/`doPatrol` reuse the already-scratch vectors (`_t1/_t2`); `reactions.applyFlinch` zeroes stray `torsoZ/headZ` when flinch dies so no frame leaks a twist.
5. **Viewmodel boot compile (F5):** compile only the equipped 2 guns at boot; the other 3 `WeaponModel`s are built but not `compileAsync`’d until first armory open.

Measured before/after: `perf-probe.ts` 600 frames/map (draw calls median/max, tris, frameMs mean/p50/p99/max, sim/render split, hudMs). Also `renderer.info.memory` + `scene.traverse` counts. Expected: Warehouse visible meshes 203→~140, total scene nodes ~360→~120 (decals), `hudMs` 0.03→0.01, p99 frameMs drops more than mean (hitch reduction).

### Headline B — Weapon weight & world read: viewmodel rework + HUD/juice/mission clarity (S2: G1/G2/G4/W1-4, A2, M2, J1, N1)  *(the “feels like a real shooter” promise)*

No kits. Instead, the two things players complain about in the first 60 seconds: the gun doesn’t feel like a heavy object, and the world doesn’t tell you where to go or whether you’re doing it right.

1. **Viewmodel inertia (G1/G2):** ADS now has a **6% overshoot + 90 ms settle** (spring), sprint lowers with a 2× faster return, fire-kick has a chamber-time tail. Camera recoil now respects ADS (×0.65). All numbers are tuned against the existing `recoilRecovery(0.155)` spring so bursts still stack climb.
2. **Viewmodel tri/segment decimation (W1/W3):** every `cyl` with radius <0.015 or hidden faces (magwells, tube insides, back-faces of rails) drops 18→8 segments; each gun merges two steel batches where finish allows. Pin: M4 47k→≤22k tris, 30→≤18 draws; AWM 52k→≤24k; M249 44→≤28 draws. A headless `armory-models` geometry budget assertion guards it.
3. **ADS alignment prove-out (W4):** headless `viewmodel-rig` test: for every catalog gun, with every legal optic and worst-case mag/Stock, project the bore line and assert ADS centre within 0.6° at `VIEWMODEL_RIG.adsDepth`.
4. **Material read pass (W3):** bump `vmScene` cool fill 0.45→0.68, add a post-fire 16-frame rim (`vmLight` +0.6, decay `exp(-dt*8)`), lower polymer metalness 0.10→0.04, raise steel scratch via `finish()`’s canvas normal variance by 12%.
5. **Kill confirm hierarchy (G6/J1):** head hitmark = diamond + louder `dink` + 6% larger crosshair pulse; body hit keeps the 4-tick cross; hitstop stays 45 ms on direct gun kills only (explosions still shake instead).
6. **World read (M2/N1):** nearest landmark name feeds the compass strip as a faint pip (“SOUK  42 m”) and the first-deploy modal lists difficulty + map + “hold RMB to aim / Q-E lean / hold X to detonate” in 3 clicks (never shows again).
