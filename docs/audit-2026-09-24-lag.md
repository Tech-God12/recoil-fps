## 1. Gunfeel — recoil, ADS, viewmodel, feedback

- **G10 (S1). All primary patterns are still placeholder `STOCK` or single-value.** `catalog.ts` base uses `STOCK [[1.1,.12]…]` for every AR/LMG and single `[1.65,-.16]` etc for others — the M4, M249 and Vector share the same spray *feel* except for multipliers. Why it matters: spray control is skill expression; identical patterns make guns feel skin-swapped. Evidence: `catalog.ts:base` + `WEAPON_CATALOG` patterns vs `recoil.ts:recoilImpulse`. **Fix (M): author per-weapon patterns (AK strong vertical + late horizontal swing, M4 flat, Vector late climb, M249 long horizontal walk). Pin TTK not changed, only pattern shape. Test: pattern length + variance.**

- **G11 (S2). Viewmodel still floats with no hands.** Only `lArm` exists during reload; idle/ADS shows floating metal (screenshots `polish/mp7.png`). Biggest "prototype" tell. Why: every AAA reference has gloves/forearms anchoring scale and recoil. **Fix (M): procedural glove boxes parented to gun per family (pistol/AR/shotgun), posed hip + ADS. Keep triangle budget low (<800 tris per hand), material simple.**

- **G12 (S2). ADS `sightY` 0.051–0.097 trusted without visual proof.** Previous fix added headless ADS geometry test (passes), but scoped FOV still hides gun back-face on 6× (`<45°` threshold). On low FOV the scoop still fills screen. Why: breaks sight picture. Evidence: `engine.ts` ADS hide threshold + `weapon-assembly.test.js`. **Fix (S): widen hide to `<38°` for ≥4× glass, add screenshot pair.**

- **G13 (S2). Camera shake is raw RNG per frame.** `composeCamera`: `eye.x += (Math.random()-0.5)*shake*0.14`. Why: jittery rather than punchy; CoD/Apex use trauma-decay with smooth noise. Feels cheap. Evidence: `engine.ts:4099-4100`. **Fix (S): replace with Perlin-like smoothed trauma shake (decaying sine, seed stable, untested currently).**

- **G14 (S3). Hitmarker timeout can still stack without fade cap.** `fx.hitmark` replaced but not pooled; rapid kills can flash too fast to read. Why: kill confirm chain should be readable at 900 RPM. Evidence: `Hud.tsx` hitmark + `App.tsx` timers. **Fix (S): queue with 120 ms cooldown, crosshair pulse +8/+14 already shipped — add numeric popup on kill.**

- **G15 (S3). No bullet crack / near-miss audio.** Enemy misses silent except distant report. 50% of "being shot at" feel is supersonic snap within ~4 m. Why: teaches direction, sells suppression. Evidence: `ai.ts` fireShot vs `audio.ts` no crack path. **Fix (M): on enemy miss within 4 m play panned crack (reuse noise burst, HPF).**

- **G16 (S3). Muzzle flash 1.2 intensity still blows out on bright desert.** Sandblast noon + flash light 8 intensity + 0.045 s — desert exposure 1.05 already high. Why: whiteout on AWM/SPAS. Evidence: `effects.ts` + `engine.ts` flash. **Fix (S): scale flash by exposure and map (desert 0.85×, town 1.0×).**

## 2. Weapon models — silhouette, proportions, materials

- **W6 (S1). Viewmodel cost is still 30–44 draws, 13k–47k tris per gun.** M4 47,217 tris / 30 draws, M249 41,676 / 44 draws — re-renders every frame at full rate. Why: main lag complaint — viewmodel alone costs more than world on integrated GPU. Evidence: `weapon-models.md` table + probe §6. **Fix (M): merge static parts per material (batch), cut cylinder segments 24→16 on non-hero silhouettes, LOD toggle on low shadow setting. Target: <18 draws, <22k tris avg. Pin budgets headless.**

- **W7 (S2). Metal vs polymer reads identical.** `finish.ts` + textures use same steel/walnut tiles with tonal variation but metalness/roughness not differentiated per piece — polymer handguards shine like steel. Why: material response is #1 cue for realism. Evidence: `weapons/finish.ts` + `core.ts` palette. **Fix (S): per-piece metalness override (receiver 0.6, polymer 0.05, wood 0.0) + roughness split, comment.**

- **W8 (S3). Selector / ejection port / mag release missing on some guns.** 1911 has correct slide, but AK selector, M4 bolt catch, SCAR charging handle not modeled. Why: gun nuts notice instantly. Evidence: `weapons/rifles.ts` geometry. **Fix (M): checklist per gun vs reference, one at a time — not this session beyond W6/W7.**

- **W9 (S3). Attachment total cost unbounded.** Each of 59 parts has draw budget, but gun+worst-case-fit not capped — kitted M249 could hit 60 draws. Why: worst-case frame after unlocking everything = slideshow. Evidence: `armory-models.test.js` per-part only. **Fix (S): assert gun + attachments ≤ 48 draws headless.**

- **W10 (S3). No LOD for distant world rifles.** Soldier `buildSoldier` rifles are lower-cost but still separate meshes per soldier × 12 enemies = 12× draw overhead at distance. Why: distant enemies contribute many draws for pixels. Evidence: `ai.ts` model. **Fix (M): soldier LOD — collapse rifle to 2 draws beyond 35 m.**

## 3. Enemy AI — readability, cover, dumb moments

- **A7 (S1). Full-squad instant knowledge still present (audited A1, not fixed).** `squad.alertAll` + `shareIntel` share exact playerPos the frame one bot sees you — Normal reaction 0.5 s × 18 squad DPS = death in ~2 s with no counterplay except instant LOS break. Why: feels like wallhacks. Evidence: `ai.ts:onEngage→alertAll`, probe squad TTK 1.6–2.2 s. **Fix (M): radio delay 1.2–2.4 s by difficulty, imperfect shared pos (+2.5 m error), test sim.**

- **A8 (S2). Intent unreadable, flanks silent.** No hand raise, no "flanking!" ping, grenade arrival has only radio line — deaths random. Why: good AI telegraphs. Evidence: `ai.ts` states no HUD cue. **Fix (M): flanker arm raise + compass ping direction.**

- **A10 (S3). `doFlank` target not nav-clamped at creation.** Raw ±16 m perpendicular geometry, `goTo` recovers via `nearestFree` but first 1.5 s path may U-turn visibly. Evidence: `ai.ts doFlank`. **Fix (S): clamp at creation.**

- **A11 (S3). PATROL allocates per tick (`tmpV.set(...).clone()`), `checkLOS` allocates `fwd`, `fireShot` allocates muzzle vectors.** Small GC churn at 20 Hz + staggered logic = frame pacing hitch. Why: death by thousand cuts on low-end. Evidence: `ai.ts`. **Fix (S): scratch vectors, assert zero-alloc harness.**

- **A12 (S3). Grenade flush through walls, no peek.** `tryGrenade` targets `lastKnown` + jitter with no arm raise; HUD arrow only <12 m. Why: silent explosion. **Fix (S): force thrower to peek/stand + spatial pin-pull.**

## 5. Graphics — lighting, shadows, fog, post, LOD

- **R6 (S1). NUM ONE LAG SOURCE: post chain + antialias + DPR 1.25 on integrated GPU.** `WebGLRenderer({antialias:true})` with `EffectComposer` (RenderPass → UnrealBloomPass (half res, 5 mips) → ShaderPass (vignette) → OutputPass) = every frame does 3 fullscreen passes even when bloom off (bloom.enabled false but still allocated). + DPR 1.25 = 1.56× pixels. + 2048 shadow texels at init before settings apply. Why: "super laggy" report — this is it. Evidence: `engine.ts:716` antialias, `728` shadow 2048 init, `composer` 4 passes. **Fix (M): main headline — disable renderer antialias, init shadow 1024, make bloom/vignette conditional (skip pass creation when off), cap DPR 1.1, add Potato preset that disables post entirely.**

- **R7 (S1). Shadows still wasteful.** Default low = 1024 but init is 2048 (first second renders at 2048 anyway), `shadowMap.autoUpdate=false` + `needsUpdate` every 10th frame (≈6 Hz) refreshes whole cascade even when nothing moved; bias -0.0004 / normalBias 0.04 tuned for 2048 but wrong for 1024/4096; no caster culling (every crate casts). Why: 2048 shadow pass costs more than world render on iGPU. Evidence: `engine.ts:749–755,4384`. **Fix (S): init 1024, tie bias to mapSize, only markNeedsUpdate if player/AI moved >0.8 m or light changed, caster whitelist (only architecture + large props).**

- **R8 (S2). Bloom abuse on desert + no adaptive tone mapping.** Exposure 1.05 fixed, `ACESFilmic` always, threshold 0.92 but strength 0.28 still blooms every bright wall at noon. Why: desert walls glow, kasbah feels muddy. Evidence: `engine.ts:722–723,819` bloom. **Fix (S): per-map exposure (desert 1.0, town 1.08, Sirocco 1.12), bloom strength 0.22 daytime / off on low, threshold 0.96.**

- **R9 (S2). Dust + motes update every frame at full N, frustumCulled false.** `Effects` dust N=320, plus `ArenaFx.motes` per warehouse, each `update` loops 320×3 floats + position.needsUpdate even when dust offscreen; 260 decal meshes each visited per frame (`visible=false` still matrix updated). Why: ~1.5 ms of JS transform cost per frame. Evidence: `effects.ts:update` + `engine.ts:3954`. **Fix (S): instance decals (2 draws), reduce dust to 180 baseline (120 on Potato), throttle dust/motes to every 2nd frame, set frustumCulled true where correct.**

- **R10 (S3). Anisotropy 8× on every material.** `maxAniso = min(8, getMaxAnisotropy)` then `anisotropy = maxAniso` per mesh. Why: 8× costs bandwidth on iGPU for marginal sharpness. Evidence: `engine.ts:788`. **Fix (S): cap at 4× baseline (8× only on High).**

- **R11 (S3). No LOD, no occlusion beyond frustum.** Decals, soldiers full detail at 80 m. Why: future cliff but not today — note.**
