# RECOIL FPS — Second audit pass, 2026-09-24 (session B)

Reviewer stance: thousands of hours of CoD, CS2, PUBG, Apex, Valorant. The player's
complaint coming into this session: **"everything is super laggy."**

This is a follow-up to `docs/audit-2026-09-24.md` (session A). Items session A
shipped (AWM/SCAR TTK, viewmodel scale, brass/smoke, hitstop, compressor, ranked
reachability, reload hint, bind list…) are **not** re-proposed. Where session A's
finding turned out stale it is corrected below (e.g. W2 "no hands" — the viewmodel
does have procedural arms, `weapons/core.ts attachArms`).

## Method (what was actually exercised)

- **No browser is obtainable in this sandbox** (verified again this session:
  Playwright's CDN, `@puppeteer/browsers` and apt all fail — no chromium/firefox
  package or download is reachable). So no screenshots this pass.
- New headless harness **`scripts/perf-probe.ts`** (+ `scripts/headless-shim.ts`,
  factored out of the existing defusal smoke test): boots the *real* Engine on all
  four maps against a stub WebGL2 context. three.js still performs every CPU-side
  step — culling, sorting, program selection, uniform upload, draw-call and
  triangle counting — so `renderer.info` numbers and JS frame times are real;
  GPU fill time is not, so draw calls / triangles / light count / fullscreen passes
  / shadow re-render rate are used as proxies.
- Scene breakdown probe (top meshes by triangles, shadow casters, light list, per
  bot mesh count), code reading of engine/AI/TDM/world/effects, and the full
  validation suite on the untouched branch (260/260 tests, 44/44 mutations, lint
  and typecheck clean — no pre-existing failures).

### Baseline numbers (before this session, `perf-probe.ts 600`)

| map | draw calls p50 / max | tris p50 / max | point lights in shader | shadow casters (tris) | JS ms/frame mean / p99 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Sandblast (alrasul) | 57 / 76 | 157 k / 267 k | 3 | 110 k | 1.52 / 7.28 |
| Town (kasbah) | 78 / 129 | 131 k / 194 k | 3 | — | 1.24 / 5.77 |
| Warehouse TDM (arena) | 203 / 236 | 102 k / 143 k | **8** | 41 k | 2.26 / 4.59 |
| Sirocco (defusal) | 120 / 172 | 82 k / 143 k | **6** | — | 1.79 / 3.92 |

Reading: CPU is not the bottleneck (≈1–2 ms of JS per frame). The lag is on the
GPU side: every lit pixel loops 3–8 point lights, the default graphics settings
push the whole frame through an off-screen post chain, and the shadow map
re-renders the entire map (including a 36 k-tri terrain) every 10th frame — a
periodic spike that reads as stutter even when the average FPS looks fine.

Severity: **S1** blocks fun · **S2** feels amateur · **S3** polish. Effort: S <2 h ·
M half-day · L multi-day.

## 1. Gunfeel

- **G1 (S2). Enemy hit reactions are identical for every hit.** `ai.ts:332` /
  `tdm.ts:474` set one scalar `flinch`; the pose only pitches the torso forward
  (`ai.ts:710`). A headshot, a leg shot and a shot from behind all look the same.
  *Why:* hit confirmation is 50 % visual; CS2/Valorant sell hits through the target's
  body. Fix (M): directional, zone-aware flinch (head snap, torso twist away from the
  shot, leg buckle). **→ shipping (headline B).**
- **G2 (S3). No per-zone hit sound on the target** beyond the head "dink". Body/limb
  hits share one tick. Fix (S): limb thud variant. Not shipping (audio mix already
  retuned last session; avoid churn).
- **G3 (S3). ADS in/out has no sway settle.** Viewmodel snaps to the sight line on
  the ADS curve with no overshoot. Fix (S). Not shipping — needs eyes on screen.
- **G4 (S3). Bolt-action forced unscope after each shot** (carried from A-G9).
  Still true; balance call, not a bug.

## 2. Weapon models

- **W1 (S2). Viewmodel scene is 66 k triangles** (probe) — more than a third of the
  median Sandblast frame. The finish shader already carries detail; cylinder
  segment counts on hidden faces are generous. Fix (M): decimate. Not shipping —
  the model suite pins shapes tightly and a decimation pass deserves a browser.
- **W2 (S2). Bots carry a full-detail finish-shader AK at any distance.**
  `models.ts buildSoldier()` builds the rifle with `GunBuilder` + `WM` finish
  materials: 5 separate meshes (darkSteel/wood/rubber/steel…) and ~7 k triangles
  per soldier, while a one-draw atlas gun (`worldWeaponMesh('rifle')`) already
  exists. At 20 m the extra detail is sub-pixel. *Why:* in TDM this is ~40 of the
  203 draw calls. Fix (S): distance LOD swap. **→ shipping (headline A).**
- **W3 (S3). Previous audit's "no hands" is stale** — procedural sleeves/gloves exist.
  They are boxy (RoundedBox gloves, 8-sided sleeves) — an art pass, not a bug.
- **W4 (S3). Dropped weapons in defusal use the atlas gun** — fine for perf, but
  there is no silhouette difference between a dropped AK and M4. Not shipping.

## 3. Enemy AI

- **A1 (S1). Every death is the same stiff plank.** `updateVisualFrame` (both
  `ai.ts:478` and `tdm.ts:1287`) rotates the *whole group* −86° about X over 0.5 s:
  the body falls backwards as a rigid board regardless of where it was shot from,
  the rifle stays glued to the hands, and the legs never bend. *Why:* kills are the
  core reward loop; a single canned fall reads "prototype" within the first minute
  and the direction of the fall is the cheapest "you did that" cue in the genre.
  Fix (L): procedural, direction/zone-driven death system. **→ shipping (headline B).**
- **A2 (S2). Corpses clip through walls.** The plank falls along the body's own
  facing with no collision test — die with your back to a wall and the torso ends
  up inside it. Fix (M): test the fall direction against `solids` and slump
  against the wall instead. **→ shipping.**
- **A3 (S2). Weapons never leave the dead soldier's hands.** Fix (S): the rifle
  drops, tumbles and lies on the floor. **→ shipping.**
- **A4 (S3). Explosion kills look like bullet kills.** Frags kill with the same
  fall. Fix: explosive variant thrown away from the blast. **→ shipping.**
- **A5 (S3). Per-tick allocations in AI hot paths.** `doPatrol` does
  `tmpV.set(...).clone()` every logic tick; `checkLOS` allocates a forward vector
  per LOS check. Cheap individually; noted as GC churn. **→ shipping (checkLOS/patrol).**
- **A6 (S3). No visible "I'm aiming at you" tell** (laser / glint for AWM bots).
  Not shipping — needs design.

## 4. Maps

- **M1 (S2). Terrain casts shadows.** Sandblast/Town terrain is a 36 k-tri merged
  mesh spanning ±260 m that `castShadow`s. It is the single biggest shadow-pass
  caster (33 % of Sandblast's shadow triangles) and its gentle 13 m undulation
  never visibly shadows anything inside the playable ±120 m. Fix (S): receive-only.
  **→ shipping.**
- **M2 (S2). Decorative point lights everywhere cost every pixel.** Warehouse spawn
  washes (2), work lights (2), map-centre lights (2) plus two always-present zero
  intensity lights = 8 point lights evaluated by every standard material fragment.
  Fix (M): a light budget that keeps a fixed small pool of real lights assigned to
  the nearest sources. **→ shipping (headline A).**
- **M3 (S3). Warehouse dust/light shafts are additive, depth-write-off cones** — fine
  per se, but combined with 8 lights they are the worst fill spot. Covered by M2.
- **M4 (S3). Landmarks still not labelled in-world** (carried). Not shipping.
- **M5 (S3). Sirocco tunnel lamps are real point lights for a 12 m interior.**
  Covered by M2's pool (they light up when you are near them, which is when they
  matter).

## 5. Graphics

- **R1 (S1). Default settings route every frame through an off-screen post chain for
  a 12 % vignette.** `DEFAULT_SETTINGS` has bloom off, grain 0, vignette 12 → 
  `postFxOn = true` → RenderPass to a full-res float target + vignette pass +
  OutputPass: two extra full-screen passes and a full-res RT write/read per frame.
  Worse, the canvas is created with `antialias: true`, but the RenderPass target
  is not multisampled — so the player pays for MSAA *and gets an aliased world*.
  Fix (S): draw the vignette as a CSS overlay and render straight to the canvas
  unless bloom/grain are on. The world gets its MSAA back for free.
  **→ shipping (headline A).**
- **R2 (S1). Periodic shadow spike = micro-stutter.** Frame max tris is 1.7× the
  median on Sandblast (267 k vs 157 k): every 10th frame re-renders the full shadow
  map. Six hitches a second is what "laggy" feels like even at 60 fps average.
  Fix: shrink the shadow pass (M1) and fit the shadow camera to the player so the
  static-map spike is cheaper and crisper. **→ shipping.**
- **R3 (S2). Shadow texels are ~15 cm on the default "low" (1024) setting** over a
  fixed ±80 m frustum → soft, crawling blobs. Fix (M): shadow frustum follows the
  player (±40 m, texel-snapped so edges don't swim). 4× the texel density at the
  same cost. **→ shipping.**
- **R4 (S3). Performance knobs don't say which ones matter.** *(Corrected during
  build: `Settings.tsx` already has one-click quality presets; the original "no
  presets" claim was wrong.)* What is missing is guidance — nothing says that
  bloom/grain switch on the whole post chain while vignette is free.
  **→ shipping (one-line "biggest FPS levers" hint in the Performance section).**
- **R5 (S3). Adaptive resolution floors at 60 % and waits 10 s of sub-45 fps before
  the first step.** Reasonable; kept. The FPS readout does not say what render
  scale is active, so the player cannot tell the scaler is working.
  **→ shipping (scale shown next to FPS).**
- **R6 (S3). Tone mapping ACES + 1.04 contrast in the vignette shader** — the
  contrast nudge disappears when post is off. Folded into exposure; no visible loss.

## 6. HUD/UI

- **U1 (S2). HUD polls engine state and re-renders the whole React tree at 20 Hz.**
  `App.tsx:237`. The engine side is cheap (0.01–0.05 ms per `hud()` call, probe),
  but React reconciliation of the full HUD at 20 Hz on a mid laptop is a few ms of
  main-thread time competing with rAF. Fix (M): split fast (crosshair/compass) and
  slow (scoreboard/feed) channels. Not shipping — needs a browser profiler to prove.
- **U2 (S2). No killcam / death recap** (carried A-J5). Not shipping (scope).
- **U3 (S3). No hit-location feedback on the shooter side** (head icon vs body tick).
  The new death variants partly cover this visually.
- **U4 (S3). FPS counter is a bare number**; see R5.
- **U5 (S3). Settings has no explanation of which knobs cost the most.** Presets + a
  one-line "biggest FPS levers" note fix this. **→ shipping.**

# Session plan (producer cut)

The player asked for **two big things**, plus "make it not laggy". So there are two
headlines this session, not one:

### Headline A — Frame Budget (performance overhaul)  *(S1: R1, R2, M1, M2, W2, F1)*
1. Light budget: every decorative point light becomes a *virtual* source; a fixed
   pool of 2 real lights is re-assigned to the nearest sources with cross-fades.
   Shader light count stops depending on the map.
2. Post chain only when it does something: vignette moves to a CSS overlay; with
   bloom/grain off the frame renders straight to the (multisampled) canvas.
3. Shadows: terrain receive-only; shadow camera follows the player, texel-snapped,
   ±40 m — cheaper spike *and* 4× sharper.
4. Bot weapon LOD: full GunBuilder rifle within 14 m, one-draw atlas gun beyond.
5. Settings guidance (presets already existed) + render-scale readout.

### Headline B — Impact & death reactions  *(S1: A1; S2: G1, A2, A3, D1, C1)*
One shared module (`src/game/reactions.ts`) used by mission AI and TDM/defusal bots:
- directional, zone-aware hit flinch (head snap, torso twist, leg buckle);
- five death variants picked from hit zone + shot direction + cause (headshot
  crumple, blown back, pitched forward, spun sideways, explosive throw), multi-joint
  keyframed with knees/arms/head, a settle bounce and a ground thud;
- wall-aware: if the fall direction is blocked within body length, the corpse
  slumps against the wall instead of clipping into it;
- the rifle drops, tumbles and lands with a clatter;
- settles once then costs nothing per frame; reset cleanly on respawn.

### Polish (ride-alongs, each measured)
P1 world MSAA restored (R1) · P2 crisper shadows (R3) · P3 perf-lever hint (R4/U5)
· P4 render-scale readout (R5) · P5 corpse wall-clip fix (A2) · P6 weapon drop +
clatter (A3/D3) · P7 body-fall thud (D1) · P8 AI hot-path allocation removal (A5)
· P9 shared death logic replaces the drifted duplicates (C1).

### Explicitly NOT doing
- **Kits/abilities** — another session owns that.
- Viewmodel decimation (W1) and arm art pass — needs a browser to judge.
- React HUD channel split (U1) — can't prove the win without a profiler.
- Killcam, mission modifiers/dailies, recorded VO — each a project of its own or
  needs assets.
- Full physics ragdoll — no physics engine in the dependency set and "add no
  dependencies" is a hard rule; the procedural system gets 90 % of the read.
