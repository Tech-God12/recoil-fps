# Sirocco map design note

Sirocco keeps its authored three-lane rectangles and bomb-site timings unchanged. The rebuild is an architectural pass on the complement mass: warm limestone, whitewash, stone plinths, stepped parapets and deep façade reveals make A Long and the residential spawn lanes read as a lived-in town. Mid uses formal paving and civic landmarks, while B tunnels deliberately switch to concrete, steel and service lighting so the route is immediately legible.

Landmarks terminate each major lane (minaret, civic structures, market and water tower), and cover is concentrated at peekable edges rather than scattered through the open lanes. Decorative dressing is kept separate from collision and uses the high-detail tier only; the low tier therefore retains identical solids, cover nodes and navigation.
