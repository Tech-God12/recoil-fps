# Recoil FPS overhaul — field brief

## What changed

- **Map presentation and narrative:** The map selector, arena cards, boot screen, and after-action paths now use a set of purpose-made dossier images for Sandblast, Town/Kasbah, Warehouse, and Sirocco. Mission cards introduce a concise operational through-line (wadi crossing → souk → extraction, or kilns → caravan court → citadel) rather than generic environment copy. The world descriptions clarify each map's clean lane identity and landmark logic.
- **New primary weapon:** The MCX-SPEAR is a 7.62×51, 20-round, 650 RPM battle rifle with a fully procedural coyote assembly: monolithic receiver, short-stroke gas system, M-LOK and Picatinny furniture, folding/telescoping stock, ambidextrous controls, open-bore flash hider, moving magazine and charging handle. It is wired through loadout, armory, defusal economy, audio, animations, attachments, tactical copy, and the shop.
- **Gun and effects quality:** The factory SPEAR and every fully equipped supported configuration pass continuous-contact checks. A dedicated reload pose brings the side charger home. Muzzle residue has an isolated, throttled, rising smoke pool, so rapid fire cannot displace hit/blood effects or obscure a player's sight picture.
- **Audio and accessibility/QoL:** Player steps now alternate heel/toe layers by sand, concrete, and wood; their mix is independently adjustable. Settings also add auto reload, safe dry-weapon quick swap, reduced motion, 75–125% tactical-map scale, and high-contrast HUD mode. Quick swap only engages when the present weapon has no ammunition and cannot reload.
- **Performance posture:** Existing staged initialization, 1.25 default pixel-ratio cap, adaptive scaling, shared texture/material cache, bounded light pool, merged map geometry, and LOD paths are preserved. The new smoke system is fixed-size and preallocated; no per-shot particle allocation is added.

## Reference grounding

## Two further major improvement areas

1. **Objective readability and live squad command.** Implement a diegetic command layer that places temporary, color-safe objective lanes and squad orders in the world/minimap, plus contextual voice barks for threats, rotations, bomb hand-offs, and extraction. It should use the existing tactical map positions and be evaluated with no-color/low-vision modes from day one.
2. **Platform-aware quality tiers and telemetry.** Add an explicit *Balanced / Quality / Competitive* preset, a short on-device scene benchmark, and opt-in local frame-time reporting. The benchmark can choose resolution/shadow/post-FX budgets for a 16 GB machine instead of making players tune separate sliders. Keep the current adaptive scaler as the safety net, and never upload telemetry without clear consent.
