# Warehouse map design note

Warehouse is balanced around a central link and two differently dressed halls: the west side is cool transit steel and the east side uses warmer timber and brick accents. Sidings provide the long rifle lane, racked halls and the link provide mid-range fights, and containers, barriers and spawn yards create short-range breaks.

Catwalks, flatcars and loading edges add readable elevation without closing nav routes. Each raised position has a ground approach and an alternate route, while spawn barriers block centre sightlines and leave multiple exits. Rail, truck and yard landmarks extend the playable identity to the perimeter without turning the boundary into a featureless wall. Decorative lights and motes remain pooled by the engine's four-light shader cap.
