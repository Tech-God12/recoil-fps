## 1. Gunfeel

- **G1 (S1). AWM needs 2 body hits at 48 RPM.** 78 dmg → 2 shots = 1.25 s TTK in
  missions, and the 74-dmg TDM cap forces **3 hits = 2.5 s TTK** in the arena.
  Why it matters: every mainstream FPS one-taps chest with a bolt sniper inside
  range; a "thunk… nothing happened" sniper feels broken, not balanced.
  Evidence: probe §2. Fix (S): raise AWM damage to 100+ (one-tap ≤150 HP pools
  only with headshot in TDM? no — one-tap body is the point: set 105 mission /
  exempt bolt guns from the 74 cap), pin TTK bands in a test.
- **G2 (S1). SCAR strictly dominates every AR.** 52 dmg = 2-shot 0.10 s body TTK;
  AK/M4 need 3. Same 600 RPM as AK, better falloff (45 m × 0.9), 20-round cost is
  no cost with infinite reserves. Why: one correct answer kills the armory game.
  Evidence: probe §2 (SCAR dps 520, TTK 0.10 s vs AK 0.20 s). Fix (S): 52→48 dmg
  (3-shot body, keeps 1-tap head + range identity), pin TTK bands.
- **G3 (S2). Viewmodels are ~2× too large.** Screenshots (`awm.png`, `ak47.png`):
  the gun fills a quarter of the screen; the AWM stock + scope occlude the entire
  lower-right. `VM_S = 1.95` plus huge hip offsets. Why: blocks sightlines, reads
  toy-like next to the world scale. Fix (M): rescale rig (~1.2–1.4), re-seat ADS
  `sightY` math, re-verify each gun's ADS alignment headless.
- **G4 (S2). Viewmodel lighting goes near-black.** `kasbah-combat.png`: the M4 is a
  black silhouette (single warm key from one side, no fill). Why: gun looks
  untextured/broken on the cool map. Fix (S): add a cool camera-side fill +
  rim to `vmScene`, verify intensities.
- **G5 (S2). No muzzle smoke, no shell casings, no impact smoke linger.**
  `effects.ts` is well pooled (28 bursts/20 tracers/200 holes) but a mag dump leaves
  no hanging smoke and no brass. Why: sustained fire feels weightless; brass is the
  cheapest "I am shooting a machine" cue in the genre. Fix (M): pooled smoke puff
  on shot + ejecting brass with gravity/floor bounce (cap ~12 alive).
- **G6 (S2). No hitstop / kill-confirm juice on the *shooter* side.** Hitmarker +
  sound exist, but no micro-freeze, no crosshair pulse, no damage number option.
  Why: kills land soft; CoD/Apex confirm kills in 3+ channels. Fix (S): 40–60 ms
  hitstop on kill + crosshair expand pulse (both already have hooks: `hit` event,
  `Reticle spread`).
- **G7 (S3). Reload hint is absolute.** HUD shows blinking RELOAD at `mag <= 5` —
  the AWM shows it at a **full** 5-round mag. Why: trains players to ignore the
  hint. Evidence: `Hud.tsx` `hud.mag <= 5`. Fix (S): `mag === 0 || mag/magSize < 0.25`.
- **G8 (S3). Tracers converge from an offset muzzle but originate oddly on lean.**
  Tracer start uses a fixed gun-side offset; fine, but with lean the skip-bubble
  (0.55 + lean×0.95) can exceed the tracer start and read as shooting through
  your own wall. Rare; note only. Fix (S): start tracers at the skip distance.
- **G9 (S3). Bolt-action forces UNSCOPED after every shot** (`rmb=false` on AWM
  fire). Authentic-ish, but with a 1.25 s cycle + re-ADS time the AWM's *felt* TTK
  is ~2 s. Revisit after G1; consider keep-ADS option. (S)

## 2. Weapon models

- **W1 (S2). M4 viewmodel is 47k triangles / 30 draws** (probe §6; M249 44 draws).
  Why: a viewmodel should be 5–15k tris; this one costs more than some *maps* did
  two years ago, and it re-renders every frame at full rate. Fix (M): decimate pass
  on cylinder segments + merge static parts per material; pin per-gun tri budgets.
- **W2 (S2). No hands/arms on the viewmodel.** Screenshots show floating guns;
  only a reload `lArm` exists and it is invisible otherwise. Why: single biggest
  "prototype" tell in every screenshot. Fix (M): simple gloved-hand rig (2 boxes +
  fingers implied) parented to gun, posed per family.
- **W3 (S3). Sight heights vary 0.051–0.097 with no alignment test.** ADS math
  trusts `sightY` per gun; nothing verifies the bore line actually meets screen
  centre per weapon/optic combo. Evidence: probe §6 sightY column. Fix (S): headless
  ADS-alignment assertion per catalog gun (project sight line, assert ≈ centre).
- **W4 (S3). Ejection port / selector / charging details inconsistent.** Changelog
  shows iterative fixes (AK lug, M4 magwell); a checklist pass per gun vs.
  reference photos is still missing (selector levers, ejection ports, mag release,
  sling points). Fix (M): checklist + photo-compare, one gun at a time. *Not this
  session beyond spot fixes — needs reference art time.*
- **W5 (S3). Attachment draw budgets are pinned but total cost is unbounded.**
  59 parts each with a budget is good; nothing caps *gun + all fitted parts*.
  A fully-kitted M249 could exceed 60 draws alone. Fix (S): assert
  gun+worst-case-fit ≤ budget headless.

## 3. Enemy AI

- **A1 (S2). Full-squad instant knowledge.** `squad.alertAll` + `shareIntel` give
  every squadmate your exact position the frame one bot sees you; combined with
  Normal reaction 0.5 s and ~18 squad DPS, being spotted in the open = death in
  ~2 s with no counterplay except breaking LOS instantly. Why: feels like
  wallhacks, not tactics. Evidence: `ai.ts` `onEngage`→`alertAll`; probe §5
  (squad-focus TTK 1.6–2.2 s). Fix (M): intel delay (radio delay 1–2.5 s by
  difficulty), imperfect shared position (last-known + error), pin with a sim test.
- **A2 (S2). Intent is unreadable.** No laser, stance change, bark timing, or HUD
  cue distinguishes PATROL/FLANK/generic ENGAGE; grenades arrive with only a radio
  line. Why: deaths feel random; good AI *telegraphs*. Fix (M): flankers raise a
  hand / callout precedes the grenade by the throw anim; add "flanking!" compass
  ping direction. (Partially exists via callouts — needs timing work.)
- **A3 (S2). Death = single fall-back tween, then frozen corpse.** `updateVisualFrame`
  rotates `rotation.x` only; no hit-direction fall, no crumple variance, no ragdoll.
  Why: every kill looks identical; ragdoll is the genre standard payoff. Fix (L):
  cheap procedural ragdoll (3-segment fall toward/away by damage dir + settle);
  *true ragdoll physics is out of scope this session*.
- **A4 (S3). `doAdvance`/`doFlank` goals are not nav-clamped at creation.**
  `flankTarget` is raw geometry (±16 m perpendicular); `goTo` recovers via
  `nearestFree`, but the first 1.5 s path may be garbage → visible U-turns.
  Evidence: `ai.ts doFlank`. Fix (S): clamp through `nearestFree` at creation.
- **A5 (S3). PATROL allocates per tick** (`tmpV.set(...).clone()`), `checkLOS`
  allocates a `fwd` vector, `fireShot` allocates muzzle vectors. Small GC churn in
  the staggered-logic path. Why: death by a thousand cuts on low-end. Fix (S):
  scratch vectors; assert zero-alloc via a counting harness if cheap.
- **A6 (S3). Grenade flush can come through walls with no tell.** `tryGrenade`
  targets `lastKnown` + jitter with no throw-anim or arm raise; the HUD grenade
  arrow only appears <12 m. Why: "exploded with no warning" deaths. Fix (S): force
  the thrower to peek/stand (visible) + play the pin-pull spatially at their position.

## 4. Maps

- **M1 (S2). Arena sightlines are symmetric but unbroken mid.** `MID_POINTS` +
  patrol logic push everyone to centre; cover density 40 nodes over 92×92 m with
  symmetric boxes. Likely fine, but *no lane timing or sightline-length audit
  exists*. Why: TDM lives or dies on mid control. Fix (M): headless lane audit
  (spawn→mid sprint seconds, longest unbroken sightline per lane) + 2–4 cover
  tweaks. *Needs iteration; start the audit this session, tune next.*
- **M2 (S3). World tri budgets drifted past the documented ceilings twice.**
  alrasul 110k (docs said 95k, tests now pin 112k), kasbah 63.7k (docs 50k, tests
  65k). Why: ceilings that move on every pass are not ceilings. Fix (S): freeze
  current pins as hard ceilings + a "budget change requires note" convention.
- **M3 (S3). Ambient life is thin.** Dust drift + clouds + flicker lights exist
  (good), but no birds, cloth/tarps motion, distant battle sounds, or moving
  traffic. Why: maps feel abandoned between contacts. Fix (M): distant-loop bed
  (sporadic far gunfire/vehicles in `playAmbient` rotation) + 2–3 animated cloth
  planes. *Audio part is cheap; cloth needs art time.*
- **M4 (S3). Landmarks exist but are never labelled in-world.** `world.landmarks`
  (water tower, souk, keep…) feed nothing — no 3D markers, no compass labels.
  Why: callouts ("souk courtyard") mean nothing until memorised. Fix (S/M): feed
  landmark bearings into the compass strip.
- **M5 (S3). Verticality is one-way.** Overlooks/routes exist for AI; players get
  stairs to upper floors but rooftop access is limited and mission sites are
  deliberately ground-level. Fine for now; note as a design ceiling, not a bug.

## 5. Graphics

- **R1 (S2). Dynamic shadows are FROZEN.** `shadowMap.autoUpdate=false` with
  `needsUpdate` only on frame ≤1 (+construction/demolition). Soldiers inserted
  after frame 1 cast **no shadows at all**; demolition is the only refresh.
  Why: characters float; grounding is half of "good graphics". Evidence:
  `engine.ts render()` + needsUpdate greps. Fix (S/M): refresh shadow map every
  Nth frame (e.g. 6) or when any actor moved >1 m; measure cost.
- **R2 (S3). Single 2048 shadow camera over ±80 m** = ~8 cm texels; acne/peter-panning
  tuned via bias, but cascade edges don't exist — distant shadows shimmer, near
  ones are blobby. Why: acceptable for stylised; note only unless R1 work extends.
- **R3 (S3). Fog + skybox seam risk on kasbah.** Fog 95–340 vs dome at 420 with
  `fog:false` — distant terrain fades to fog colour, dome shows behind; horizon
  banding possible at dawn. Verify visually when a browser exists.
- **R4 (S3). No LOD/culling beyond frustum.** 260 pooled decal meshes + 60 blood
  meshes are always in the graph (visible=false still traversed); soldiers are
  full-detail at 200 m. Why: fine today (~250 draws), but a profile cliff later.
  Fix (S): `layers`/`frustumCulled` audit + distance-hide for decals; soldier LOD
  is a later L.
- **R5 (S3). Post chain always compiles bloom.** `UnrealBloomPass` is constructed
  and `composer.setSize` runs even when `postFxOn=false` renders direct — plus
  `vignettePass.enabled=false` still costs a pass slot. Minor; the direct-render
  path already skips passes. Note only.

## 6. HUD/UI

- **U1 (S1). Ranked mode is 100% unreachable + would crash.** Menu shows
  OPERATION BLACKOUT (`Screens.tsx` item 03) calling `onRanked?.()`, but `App`
  never passes `onRanked`, nothing sets phase `'ranked-setup'`, `RankedSetup` /
  `rankFor` are unimported (typecheck fails), and comp launch would crash in
  `start()` (`missionRuntime` undefined), `hud()` mission block, and the debrief
  (missing `tdm.outcome`). Thousands of lines of comp code are dead in production.
  Why: a menu button that does nothing is the worst UI lie; ranked is also the
  only progression endgame. Fix (M): wire menu→setup→launch→debrief→requeue,
  fix the 4 crash/type sites, block streak keys in ranked, mode-preserving
  restart. **Headline candidate.**
- **U2 (S2). Pause → Restart silently changes modes.** `onRestart` calls `deploy()`
  which launches `'mission'` — from a comp match (once reachable) or TDM setup
  you'd restart into the wrong mode (arena+mission ⇒ TDM). Evidence: `App.tsx`
  resume/restart handlers. Fix (S, with U1): restart preserves mode+map.
- **U3 (S2). Threat readout is a wallhack-lite with no direction.** `hud.nearest`
  shows the closest hostile <30 m through walls, centred (not directional).
  Why: free intel with no counterplay; also overlaps the crosshair. Fix (S):
  only show if recently visible (`lastSeenT < 3`) or damaged-you-recently, and
  offset it directionally.
- **U4 (S2). Key-binds reference omits half the game.** BINDS lacks B (masterkey),
  V/T/brackets (optics), Tab (scoreboard), 3–7 (streaks), Q-tap vs Q-hold nuance.
  The onboarding strip covers 12 s, then nothing. Why: undiscoverable depth.
  Evidence: `Settings.tsx` BINDS vs `engine.ts` bindings. Fix (S): complete the
  list + a "?" overlay in pause.
- **U5 (S3). Menu keyboard nav can't reach item 5.** `setSel((s+1)%4)` with 5 items
  — SETTINGS unreachable by keyboard; modulo should be 5. Evidence: `Screens.tsx`
  TacticalHome. Fix (S): one-line + test.
- **U6 (S3). Kill feed stringly-typed.** Non-TDM feed packs `YOU  [W]  name` into
  one string and re-splits on double spaces (`Hud.tsx`) — breaks on any name with
  double spaces, untranslatable. Fix (S): structured `{killer, weapon, victim}`.
- **U7 (S3). TDM respawn bar hardcodes `/5`.** Matches `TDM_RESPAWN_SECONDS` today;
  drifts tomorrow. Fix (S): pass the constant through `hud()`.
- **U8 (S3). Staged screenshots show an empty black bar** (`enemy-fire.png`,
  `awm.png` top-centre) where the controls strip should be — conditional content
  renders an empty container. Fix (S): don't render the strip with no content.
  (Verify against current `Hud.tsx` — onboarding strip is mission-gated; the shots
  predate it. If already fixed, close with a comment.)

## 7. Audio

- **D1 (S2). No master compressor.** Each gunshot stacks bursts at gains
  1.0+0.68+0.42 into a 0.85 master with no `DynamicsCompressor` — sustained fire +
  explosion + callouts hard-clip. Why: clipping reads as "cheap web game" instantly.
  Evidence: `audio.ts ensure()` graph. Fix (S): compressor + makeup gain, pinned
  by a node-graph unit test.
- **D2 (S2). No bullet cracks / near-miss snaps.** Enemy misses are silent except
  the distant report; there is no supersonic crack when rounds pass the camera.
  Why: the crack is 50% of "being shot at" feel and teaches direction. Fix (M):
  on enemy miss within ~4 m of the eye, play a crack with pan by miss side.
- **D3 (S3). Missing layers: ricochets, shell casings, enemy footsteps, breathing.**
  Player footsteps + traps exist; enemies are silent movers; no casing tink on
  reload/fire; no ricochet whine on metal. Why: each is a small realism sale.
  Fix (M, piecemeal): casings with fire (cheap: reuse noise burst + tiny delay),
  enemy footstep radius (needs AI anim hook), ricochet on metal impacts.
- **D4 (S3). Voice = OS text-to-speech.** Robot announcer with per-machine voices;
  enemy barks are TTS too. Why: inconsistent quality, can feel placeholder; but
  zero-asset and it *works*. Keep; note as asset-ceiling, not a bug.
- **D5 (S3). `spatialVoices` map expiry.** `expires` timestamps exist — verify
  cleanup actually runs (leak = dead panners accumulate over long matches).
  Evidence: `audio.ts:14`. Fix (S): read + test, prune in `updateListener`.

## 8. Game feel & juice

- **J1 (S2). Nuke is mathematically decorative.** 2500 pts = 25 kills / 17 headshots
  in ONE life (points reset on death); a 150 s TDM cannot produce that, missions
  rarely do. Chopper (1500) is nearly as bad. Why: a whole ladder tier players
  never touch might as well not exist. Evidence: `streaks.ts` costs vs
  `STREAK_POINTS`. Fix (S): re-cost ladder (e.g. 300/600/850/1200/2000) + faster
  claim feedback; pin earnability sim (X kills → Y streaks).
- **J2 (S2). Victory/defeat sequences are thin.** Win = voice line + 0.6 s delay +
  results screen; no slow-mo final kill, no music sting, no MVP framing. Why:
  the emotional payoff of 10 minutes is a spreadsheet. Fix (M): final-kill slow-mo
  (0.3× for 1.2 s) + sting + graded stamp animation (stamp exists — animate it).
- **J3 (S3). Screen shake is RNG-per-frame.** `composeCamera` uses raw
  `Math.random()` offsets — jittery rather than punchy, and unseeded (untestable).
  Fix (S): trauma-based shake (decaying noise w/ smooth curve) driven by the
  existing `shake` value.
- **J4 (S3). Streak callouts can collide with mission banners** (`fx.banner &&
  !fx.missionBanner` hides streaks during objectives — the nuke banner can be
  swallowed). Fix (S): queue banners, never drop.
- **J5 (S3). No killcam, no death recap.** Death shows damage arcs only; no
  "killed by X with Y from Zm" recap. TDM/comp feeds partially cover it. Note as
  next-session M.

## 9. Onboarding & UX

- **N1 (S2). First 60 s: settings never surface.** Difficulty/map live in Settings;
  the menu deploys immediately. New players get Normal/alrasul with no briefing of
  controls beyond a 12 s strip. Why: first-session confusion = churn. Fix (S):
  first-deploy modal: difficulty + map + "hold RMB to aim" in 3 clicks.
- **N2 (S2). Armory has no SR/role guidance.** 10 guns × 59 parts with stat bars
  but no "start here" path; prices ($63k catalog) vs ~$1–2k/run earnings = ~40 runs
  to full unlock with no intermediate goals. Why: analysis paralysis + hopeless
  grind perception. Fix (M): recommended-first-buy badges + per-run earnings
  projection ("3 more runs → AK-47").
- **N3 (S3). Boot screen is indeterminate.** Honest (per changelog) but a cold
  `buildWorld` takes ~450 ms + PMREM + compile — staged across frames already.
  Fine; note only.
- **N4 (S3). `level = 13 + missions` is arbitrary.** Starts at "LVL 13" for no
  reason. Why: fake-feeling progression. Fix (S): derive from XP (kills + missions
  + wins) with a documented curve, or drop the number.

## 10. Meta & replayability

- **P1 (S1). No reason to play a 5th time (missions).** Fixed phases, fixed
  insertions, no randomization, no challenges/dailies, no score chase (score is
  per-run, unpersisted except wallet). Why: the core loop is one-and-done.
  Fix (M/L): phase-5 plan — seeded insertion shuffle + weekly challenge modifiers
  (pistol-only pays ×2, etc.) + persisted best-score/grade per mission. *This
  session: ship persisted best grades + insertion shuffle (M); dailies next.*
- **P2 (S2). Ranked ladder exists but is dead (see U1).** The entire retention
  endgame (rating, placements, divisions) ships zero value until wired. Covered
  by headline.
- **P3 (S3). Profile stats are thin.** kills/missions/cash only — no accuracy,
  headshots, wins, streaks, per-weapon kills. `settleResult` has the data; it just
  isn't kept. Fix (S): persist a stats block, show on profile card.
- **P4 (S3). Difficulty curve is flat inside a mission.** Pressure targets per
  phase (3/6/6/9/6) but enemy accuracy/reaction don't ramp with player performance.
  Admirable restraint, honestly; note only — adaptive difficulty is a trap.

## 11. Performance & robustness

- **F1 (S2). HUD re-renders every 50 ms with 73 compass nodes + full tree.** Works,
  but every poll re-renders radar/footers/ammo. Why: React churn on the critical
  path; low-end jank. Fix (S): memoize static sub-trees (`MissionObjective`,
  compass ticks), split poll rates (20 Hz vitals, 4 Hz radar).
- **F2 (S3). `hud()` allocates per poll** (`pings.map`, `enemiesMap.map/filter`,
  roster spreads) — minor GC churn at 20 Hz. Fix (S): reuse buffers or accept;
  measure first.
- **F3 (S3). Bullet holes/blood = 260 scene-graph nodes.** Individually toggled
  meshes; an InstancedMesh pair would be 2 draws. Why: graph traversal cost +
  260 matrix updates. Fix (M): instance them.
- **F4 (S3). Resize/tab-away/context-loss are handled** (resize listener, blur→pause,
  context lost/restored with messaging). Verified by reading. No action.
- **F5 (S3). Adaptive resolution is well-designed** (ladder + lock + median FPS).
  `ADAPT_STALL_SECONDS` + windows look right. No action; needs real-GPU validation.

## 12. Code health (player-facing)

- **C1 (S2). `damagePlayerTDM` guesses headshots by damage (`amount >= 40`).**
  Frag/streak/explosion damage ≥40 is reported as a headshot to feed + stats.
  Why: lying kill feed + wrong HS stats. Evidence: `engine.ts damagePlayerTDM`.
  Fix (S): thread an explicit `isHead` through (frag/streak → false).
- **C2 (S2). Streak keys are live in ranked.** `onKeyDown` activates streaks with
  no `isComp` guard; ranked targets resolve to the empty mission roster, so
  sentry/airstrike fizzle or only hurt the player. Fix (S, with U1): ignore streak
  keys + hide rail in comp (rail already hidden; keys aren't).
- **C3 (S3). Own-frag self-damage in TDM.** Player frags (`!g.owner`) damage the
  thrower; bot frags spare teammates. Inconsistent; CoD-style would spare self or
  clarify. Fix (S): decide + document (spare self, keep risk for stuck/cooked? —
  simplest: spare self).
- **C4 (S3). TDM respawn uses magic `5` in HUD vs constant in engine.** Covered by U7.
- **C5 (S3). `CASH_REASONS` lacks `BLACKOUT`.** Ranked earnings have no debrief row
  (comp path shows no cash breakdown at all). Fix (S, with U1): add row + show
  wallet delta in `CompDebriefPanel`.
- **C6 (S3). Two TDM entry paths (`mode:'tdm'` vs arena-implied) + `launch` map/mode
  matrix is confusing.** `deploy()` on arena ⇒ TDM via mapId; `launch('arena','tdm')`
  explicit. Works, but the next dev will break it. Fix (S): single `resolveMode()`
  helper + test.
- **C7 (S3). Mutation harness + 200 tests are genuinely good.** The suite caught
  real regressions (changelog shows pins). Keep investing; the one red test on
  main must be triaged (see §0), not left red.
